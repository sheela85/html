1.Acting career

1990-1998


1.Ajith began his acting career through a one-scene appearance as a school child in En Veedu En Kanavar (1990).

2.Through the recommendation of S. P. Balasubrahmanyam, whose son, S. P. Charan, was a classmate of his, he was cast in his first lead role in the Telugu romantic drama Prema Pusthakam (1993), which remains his only Telugu film to date.

3.However, the shoot of the film was stalled soon after production began, following the death of the film's director, Gollapudi Srinivas.

4.[21] As a result, his first major theatrical release was the Tamil romantic drama Amaravathi (1993), which Ajith had signed after being recommended to director Selva by Balasubrahmanyam, who had seen parts of Prema Pusthakam prior to its delay.

5.[22] As the film went into post-production, Ajith was bed-ridden due to a racing injury while training for an amateur motor race.

2.Racing career


1.Besides his acting career, Ajith is also an occasional racing driver, competing in circuits around India in places such as Mumbai, Chennai and Delhi.

2.He is one among very few Indians to race in the International arena and in FIA championships.

3.He has also been abroad for various races, including Germany and Malaysia.

4.He drove in the 2003 Formula Asia BMW Championships.

5.[8] He raced in the 2010 Formula 2 Championship along with two other Indians, Armaan Ebrahim and Parthiva Sureshwaren.[9]

Formula BMW Asia (2003)

1.After a one-off race during the Formula Maruti Indian Championships in 2002, where he finished in fourth place, Ajith signed a contract with manager Akbar Ebrahim, confirming his participation in the inaugural Formula BMW Asia championship.

2.Despite spinning out in the first lap of his first race, Ajith successfully completed the season by finishing twelfth.[84]
